# App Store Badge Upload Folder

Drop the official app store badge images here.

| Filename | Description |
|---|---|
| `badge-appstore.svg` | Apple App Store badge (download from Apple) |
| `badge-playstore.svg` | Google Play badge (download from Google) |

Update `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_STORE_URL` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_PLAY_STORE_URL` in `.env.local` with your published app links.
